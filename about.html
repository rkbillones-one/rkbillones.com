About

Robert Kerwin C. Billones, Ph.D.

Technology Strategy · Industrial AI & Data · Advanced Manufacturing & Robotics

Dr. Robert Kerwin C. Billones is an engineer and applied researcher who turns research into working technology for industry and government. He is a full professor in the Department of Biomedical, Manufacturing, and Robotics Engineering at De La Salle University (DLSU), Manila, and a Research Fellow of the Intelligent Systems Laboratory Research Unit of DLSU’s Center for Engineering and Sustainable Development Research (CESDR). He brings 15+ years of research and development experience, beginning as a digital IC design engineer at ROHM LSI Design Philippines.

He is co-founder and CEO of Intelligent Systems Innovation (ISI) Inc., a DLSU spin-off in AI and data. ISI commercializes CATCH-ALL, an AI visual analytics platform for mobility and traffic that grew out of a DOST-funded research and a Philippine patent. CATCH-ALL was piloted by DLSU in Manila City, with the MMDA, in Makati City and in Roxas City, and deployed commercially by ISI in General Santos City and the Cordillera region. He personally led product development and managed the projects and client relationships, including workplace-safety AI for a multinational consumer-goods company in the Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand. In November 2023 he joined the Philippine Investment Mission to Japan under the DTI–JICA Industrial Human Resource Development project. ISI’s partners include AWS, the NVIDIA Inception Program and the Intel Partner Alliance.

His applied research spans AI, computer vision, cyber-physical systems, digital twins, Industry 4.0 and optimization, with 110+ international publications. He led the DOST-PCIEERD Digital Manufacturing Assembly Trainer with Digital Twin Simulator, which integrates digital twin technologies with physical manufacturing assets and includes an offline retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistant that guides users through digital manufacturing workflows. He will also lead an approved DOST-funded project for 2028–2029, to be implemented by De La Salle University, on AI-enabled machine-level digital twins, a real-time warehouse location system and building information management.

Building on this technical work, he also conducts technology-based policy research: he is Asia Coordinator for AI Governance of the Asia-Europe for Artificial Intelligence (AE4AI) Network, a research group member of the AI Policy Clinic of the Center for AI and Digital Policy (CAIDP), and an expert member of ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 – Artificial Intelligence through the Philippine standards committee. He designed and led the AI Governance for Higher Education Institutions in Asia workshop series of the United Board for Christian Higher Education in Asia (2026), and co-authored the AE4AI white paper for universities on AI governance, also published in the Singapore Management University Centre for Digital Law research paper series. He co-edited the open-access Springer Nature proceedings of ASEFInnoLab6, The Role of Universities in an AI-Driven Future (2026).

He holds a Ph.D. in Electronics and Communications Engineering (DLSU, 2018) and is a Leaders in Innovation Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering, United Kingdom.

Through the DLSU School of Lifelong Learning, he designs and delivers executive and technical training: the Industry 4.0 Bootcamp for DOST regional offices, a Certificate Course on Augmented Reality and Digital Twins for electronics and semiconductor manufacturing companies, Machine Learning for Statisticians and Researchers for a Philippine financial regulator, and Mathematics for Machine Learning for a leading fintech company. Each program ends with participant case-study projects.
