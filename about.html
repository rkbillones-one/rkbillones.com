About

Robert Kerwin C. Billones, Ph.D.

Technology Strategy · Industrial AI & Data · Advanced Manufacturing & Robotics

Dr. Robert Kerwin C. Billones is an engineer and applied researcher who turns research into working technology for industry and government. He is a full professor in the Department of Biomedical, Manufacturing, and Robotics Engineering at De La Salle University (DLSU), Manila, and a Research Fellow of the Intelligent Systems Laboratory Research Unit of DLSU’s Center for Engineering and Sustainable Development Research (CESDR). He brings 15+ years of research and development experience, beginning as a digital IC design engineer at ROHM LSI Design Philippines.

He is co-founder and CEO of Intelligent Systems Innovation (ISI) Inc., a DLSU spin-off in AI and data. ISI commercializes CATCH-ALL, an AI visual analytics platform for mobility and traffic that grew out of his DOST-funded research and a Philippine patent. CATCH-ALL was piloted by DLSU in Manila City, with the MMDA, in Makati City and in Roxas City, and deployed commercially by ISI in General Santos City and the Cordillera region. He personally led product development and managed the projects and client relationships, including workplace-safety AI for Unilever in the Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand. ISI’s partners include AWS, the NVIDIA Inception Program, the Intel Partner Alliance and Profet AI.

His applied research spans AI, computer vision, cyber-physical systems, digital twins, Industry 4.0 and optimization, with 110+ international publications. He led the DOST-PCIEERD Digital Manufacturing Assembly Trainer with Digital Twin Simulator and is preparing a pipeline project for 2028–2029 on AI-enabled digital twins for injection molding, real-time warehouse location tracking and building energy intelligence, with a Philippine plastics manufacturer and DOST-MIRDC as cooperating partners. Building on this technical work, he also conducts technology-based policy research: he is Asia Coordinator for AI Governance of the Asia-Europe for Artificial Intelligence (AE4AI) Network, a research group member of the AI Policy Clinic of the Center for AI and Digital Policy (CAIDP), and an expert member of ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 – Artificial Intelligence through the Philippine standards committee. He holds a Ph.D. in Electronics and Communications Engineering (DLSU, 2018) and is a Leaders in Innovation Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering, United Kingdom.
