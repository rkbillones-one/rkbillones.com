From lab to market

Research that became a product, used in the field

Our vision-based AI research led to a Philippine patent and to CATCH-ALL, an AI visual analytics platform now commercialized by ISI Inc. CATCH-ALL was first proven in pilot projects in Manila City, with the MMDA, in Makati City and in Roxas City, implemented by De La Salle University with the support of ISI and funded by the Department of Science and Technology. ISI then deployed it commercially in General Santos City and the Cordillera region. I personally led product development and managed the projects and client relationships. The same team went on to build workplace-safety AI for a multinational consumer-goods company in the Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand.
